## Getting Started

### Account Setup

1. **Receive Invitation**: You'll receive an email invitation from your fund administrator
2. **Create Password**: Click the link and set up your secure password
3. **Complete KYC**: Upload required identification documents (if applicable)
4. **Login**: Access the platform at your fund's URL

### First Login

After logging in, you'll see:
- **Dashboard**: Personalized overview based on your role
- **Navigation Menu**: Left sidebar with available modules
- **Notifications**: Bell icon for important updates
- **Profile**: Top-right corner for settings

### Navigation Tips

- Use the **search bar** to quickly find products or transactions
- **Bookmarks**: Star frequently viewed pages
- **Export**: Download data as CSV/Excel from any table
- **Filters**: Refine data views using column filters

## Investor Guide

### Dashboard Overview

Your investor dashboard displays:
- **Portfolio Value**: Total current value with change indicators
- **Asset Allocation**: Pie chart showing distribution across asset classes
- **Performance Chart**: Line graph of portfolio value over time
- **Recent Transactions**: Latest buys, sells, and dividends
- **Holdings Summary**: Table of all your investments

### Viewing Your Portfolio

#### Portfolio Summary
Navigate to **Portfolio > Summary** to see:
- Total invested capital
- Current market value
- Unrealized gains/losses
- YTD return percentage
- IRR (Internal Rate of Return)

#### Holdings Detail
Click **Portfolio > Holdings** for:
- Individual product positions
- Quantity owned (units/tokens)
- Average cost basis
- Current NAV per unit
- Market value per position
- % allocation

**Actions Available**:
- Click any holding for detailed analysis
- Export holdings to Excel
- Set price alerts

### Transaction History

Access **Portfolio > Transactions** to view:
- All purchases and sales
- Dividend payments received
- Token transfers (if applicable)
- Fee deductions
- Status indicators (Pending, Completed, Failed)

**Filter Options**:
- Date range
- Transaction type
- Product
- Status

### Making Investments

#### Browse Products
Go to **Invest > Products** to explore:
- Available investment opportunities
- Product details (strategy, risk level, minimum investment)
- Historical performance
- ESG ratings
- Fact sheets (downloadable PDF)

#### Subscribe to a Product
1. Click **Invest** on desired product
2. Enter investment amount
3. Review terms and conditions
4. Confirm transaction
5. Receive confirmation email

*Note: Some products may have lock-up periods or subscription windows.*

### Tokenized Assets

If you hold tokenized assets:

#### View Tokens
Navigate to **Tokens > My Tokens**:
- Token balances
- Current token price
- 24h change
- Staking rewards (if applicable)

#### Transfer Tokens
1. Go to **Tokens > Transfer**
2. Select token
3. Enter recipient wallet address
4. Specify amount
5. Review network fees
6. Confirm transfer

*Ensure recipient address is correct - blockchain transactions are irreversible.*

#### Claim Dividends
When dividends are distributed:
- Notification appears in dashboard
- Go to **Tokens > Dividends**
- Click **Claim** to receive payment
- Funds appear in your cash balance

### Reports & Statements

Access **Reports** section for:
- **Monthly Statements**: Portfolio summary and transactions
- **Tax Documents**: 1099, capital gains reports
- **Performance Reports**: Detailed analytics
- **ESG Reports**: Sustainability impact metrics

**Schedule Reports**:
- Set up automatic monthly email delivery
- Choose format (PDF, Excel)
- Select recipients

## Analyst Guide

### Market Analytics Dashboard

Your analyst dashboard includes:
- **Market Overview**: Global emerging markets indices
- **Sector Performance**: Heatmap by industry
- **Risk Metrics**: VaR, volatility, correlation matrix
- **Economic Indicators**: GDP growth, inflation, interest rates

### Product Analysis

#### Comparative Analysis
Go to **Analytics > Compare Products**:
1. Select 2-10 products
2. Choose metrics (return, risk, Sharpe, etc.)
3. View side-by-side comparison table
4. Generate scatter plot (risk vs return)
5. Export comparison report

#### Performance Attribution
Navigate to **Analytics > Attribution**:
- Returns breakdown by:
  - Asset allocation effect
  - Security selection effect
  - Currency effect
  - Fees impact

### Risk Analytics

#### Risk Dashboard
Access **Analytics > Risk**:
- **VaR (Value at Risk)**: Maximum expected loss at confidence levels
- **Stress Testing**: Portfolio impact under various scenarios
- **Liquidity Analysis**: Redemption capacity
- **Concentration Risk**: Exposure by country, sector, issuer

#### Create Custom Reports
1. Go to **Analytics > Report Builder**
2. Select data sources
3. Choose metrics and dimensions
4. Apply filters
5. Design visualization
6. Save or schedule report

### Benchmarking

Compare products against benchmarks:
- **MSCI Emerging Markets Index**
- **J.P. Morgan EMBI** (for bonds)
- **Custom benchmarks**

**Steps**:
1. Select product
2. Choose benchmark
3. Set time period
4. View:
   - Tracking error
   - Information ratio
   - Alpha/Beta
   - Up/down capture

### Data Export

All analytics can be exported:
- **CSV/Excel**: Raw data for further analysis
- **PDF**: Formatted reports for presentations
- **API Access**: For programmatic data retrieval (contact IT)

## Fund Manager Guide

### AUM Overview

Your fund manager dashboard shows:
- **Total AUM**: Aggregate assets across all products
- **AUM Trend**: Graph over time with inflows/outflows
- **Product Performance**: List of products with returns
- **Fee Accruals**: Pending management and performance fees

### Product Management

#### Create New Product
1. Navigate to **Products > Create**
2. Fill in product details:
   - Name and description
   - Asset class
   - Target country/region
   - Base currency
   - Initial capital
3. Configure fees:
   - Management fee %
   - Performance fee %
   - Hurdle rate (if applicable)
   - High water mark setting
4. Set investment parameters:
   - Minimum investment
   - Lock-up period
   - Subscription frequency
5. Submit for approval (if required)

#### Update Product Details
- Edit strategy description
- Update key personnel
- Modify risk rating
- Add/remove holdings
- Adjust ESG scores

### Fee Management

#### Calculate Fees
Go to **Fees > Calculate**:
1. Select product(s)
2. Choose calculation date
3. Review inputs (AUM, returns)
4. Run calculation
5. Review results:
   - Management fee
   - Performance fee (with HWM check)
   - Total fees
6. Approve and post

#### Fee Reports
Access **Fees > Reports**:
- Accrual schedules
- Payment history
- Investor fee allocations
- Revenue forecasts

### Tokenization

#### Launch Tokenized Product
1. Go to **Tokens > Create Token**
2. Link to existing product
3. Configure token:
   - Name and symbol
   - Total supply
   - Decimals
   - Lock-up settings
4. Deploy smart contract
5. Distribute tokens to investors

#### Manage Corporate Actions
**Dividend Distribution**:
1. Navigate to **Tokens > Dividends**
2. Select token
3. Enter dividend per token
4. Set record date and payment date
5. Review total distribution amount
6. Execute distribution

**Token Minting/Burning**:
- Mint new tokens for subscriptions
- Burn tokens for redemptions
- Requires compliance approval

### Investor Relations

#### Communication Tools
- **Announcements**: Post updates to all investors
- **Newsletters**: Create and distribute periodic reports
- **Webinar Integration**: Schedule investor calls

#### Subscription/Redemption Management
Review pending requests:
- Approve/reject subscriptions
- Process redemption requests
- Monitor lock-up compliance
- Generate capital call notices

## Admin Guide

### User Management

#### View Users
Navigate to **Admin > Users**:
- Search by name, email, or role
- Filter by status (Active, Inactive, Suspended)
- View last login date
- See assigned roles

#### Create New User
1. Click **Add User**
2. Enter details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Temporary password
   - Assigned role
3. Set permissions (if custom role)
4. Send invitation email

#### Update User Role
1. Select user
2. Click **Edit Roles**
3. Add/remove roles
4. Save changes
5. User receives notification

#### Suspend/Activate User
- **Suspend**: Immediately revoke access
- **Activate**: Restore access
- Reason for suspension is logged

### Role & Permission Management

#### View Roles
Go to **Admin > Roles**:
- System-defined roles (Super Admin, Fund Manager, etc.)
- Custom roles
- Permission assignments

#### Create Custom Role
1. Click **Create Role**
2. Enter role name and description
3. Select permissions:
   - Products: Create, Read, Update, Delete
   - Tokens: Create, Transfer, Distribute
   - Users: Manage, View only
   - Fees: Calculate, Approve, View
   - Analytics: Full access, Read-only
   - Admin: User management, System settings
4. Save role
5. Assign to users

### Audit Logs

Access **Admin > Audit Logs**:
- Complete history of system actions
- Filter by:
  - User
  - Action type (Create, Update, Delete, Login)
  - Resource type
  - Date range
  - IP address

**Export Logs**:
- Download for compliance reviews
- Scheduled automated exports
- Integration with SIEM systems

### System Settings

#### General Configuration
- Platform name and branding
- Default currency
- Timezone settings
- Session timeout duration

#### Email Templates
- Customize notification emails
- HTML templates for reports
- Sender configuration

#### API Keys
- Generate API keys for integrations
- Set permissions per key
- Monitor usage
- Revoke compromised keys

### Backup & Recovery

#### Manual Backup
Go to **Admin > Backups**:
1. Click **Create Backup**
2. Select components:
   - Database
   - File storage
   - Configuration
3. Download backup file
4. Store securely offline

#### Restore from Backup
*Requires Super Admin approval*
1. Upload backup file
2. Verify backup integrity
3. Select restore point
4. Confirm restoration
5. System restarts with restored data

## Compliance Officer Guide

### KYC/AML Dashboard

Your compliance dashboard shows:
- **Pending Reviews**: Investors awaiting verification
- **Expiring Documents**: IDs requiring renewal
- **Alerts**: Suspicious activity flags
- **Geographic Risk**: Exposure to high-risk jurisdictions

### Investor Verification

#### Review KYC Submissions
Navigate to **Compliance > KYC Reviews**:
1. Select pending submission
2. Review documents:
   - Government ID
   - Proof of address
   - Source of wealth declaration
3. Verify information
4. Approve or reject with reason
5. Investor notified automatically

#### Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)
For high-risk investors:
- Additional documentation required
- Senior compliance approval needed
- Ongoing monitoring enhanced

### Transaction Monitoring

#### AML Alerts
Access **Compliance > AML Monitoring**:
- Large transaction alerts (> $10,000)
- Unusual pattern detection
- Sanctions screening matches
- PEP (Politically Exposed Person) flags

#### Investigate Alerts
1. Click alert for details
2. Review transaction history
3. Request additional information from investor
4. Document findings
5. Resolution:
   - Clear alert (false positive)
   - File SAR (Suspicious Activity Report)
   - Freeze account (if required)

### Regulatory Reporting

#### Generate Reports
Go to **Compliance > Reports**:
- **CTR**: Currency Transaction Reports
- **SAR**: Suspicious Activity Reports
- **FATCA/CRS**: Tax compliance reporting
- **Local Regulations**: Country-specific filings

#### Submission Workflow
1. Generate report
2. Review for accuracy
3. Obtain required approvals
4. Submit to regulator
5. Archive confirmation

### Policy Management

#### Update Compliance Policies
1. Navigate to **Compliance > Policies**
2. Upload new policy document
3. Set effective date
4. Require acknowledgment from users
5. Track completion

#### Training Records
- View employee training completion
- Schedule mandatory training
- Generate compliance training reports

## FAQs & Support

### Common Questions

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email, and we'll send a reset link.

**Q: Can I access the platform on mobile?**
A: Yes, the platform is fully responsive. Access via your mobile browser.

**Q: How long do withdrawals take?**
A: Standard redemptions process in 3-5 business days. Token transfers are near-instant.

**Q: Where can I find tax documents?**
A: Go to Reports > Tax Documents. Documents are available after year-end closing.

**Q: How do I contact support?**
A: Use the Help widget (bottom-right) or email support@em-platform.com.

### Security Best Practices

1. **Use strong passwords**: Minimum 12 characters, mix of letters, numbers, symbols
2. **Enable 2FA**: Add an extra layer of security
3. **Don't share credentials**: Keep your login information private
4. **Log out**: Always log out when finished, especially on shared devices
5. **Verify URLs**: Ensure you're on the official platform URL
6. **Report suspicious activity**: Contact support immediately if you notice anything unusual
